US admin hire vs. Filipino executive assistant: true cost & ROI.

When a growing US company finally admits the founder needs daily administrative leverage, the first instinct is often posting a local $60,000 W-2 admin role. Before adding $75k+ in fixed payroll, look closely at the real fully loaded math—and where each option wins.

What belongs with a remote Filipino executive assistant

Every workflow that happens inside email, calendars, cloud docs, CRMs, and chat tools translates seamlessly to an embedded US-hours Filipino partner:

  • Inbox triage & drafted replies: Turning 120 noisy daily emails into 8 clean founder decisions waiting for approval.
  • Calendar defense & scheduling: Protecting deep-work blocks, coordinating client calls, and sending crisp pre-meeting briefs.
  • Pipeline & vendor follow-through: Chasing unsigned quotes, updating HubSpot/Jobber/Salesforce, and tracking open action items.
  • SOPs & operational hygiene: Turning messy repeatable processes into clean documented playbooks your entire team relies on.

What should stay with a local US hire

Smart founders do not outsource everything blindly. Keep work in-house with your local US team whenever it requires physical site visits, licensed estimating or legal authority, face-to-face closing conversations, or hands-on warehouse/office presence.

In fact, many US founders use the ~$50,000/year saved on admin overhead to hire a stronger local revenue generator—while their Filipino executive assistant keeps both leaders organized every single day.
