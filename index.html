Founder story

Built from 15 years of real freelance work.

Agentic Filipino was built by Cheyne Casino, after spending 15 years in the freelance world and seeing the work from the inside.

Over the years, he saw the same problem again and again: great businesses needed dependable talent, and great Filipino professionals needed better opportunities, clearer leadership, and a team that actually knew how to match both sides well.

What started as freelance experience became a bigger mission. Agentic Filipino was created to help companies hire with more confidence, and to help Filipino talent step into serious long-term work where they can grow, contribute, and be valued.

The goal is simple: build strong teams, create real opportunities, and make outsourcing feel more human, reliable, and high-quality.
