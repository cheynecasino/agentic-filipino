Executive Support Guide

What to look for in a remote executive assistant.

Many people can do admin tasks. Far fewer can create real leverage for a founder. The best remote executive assistants bring communication strength, ownership, and the ability to protect time without creating more management overhead.

Best fit: founders and operators hiring executive support for the first time or replacing weak admin support.

Communication comes first

The best executive assistants communicate clearly, ask useful questions, and keep follow-through visible. Without that, even strong tool skills will not matter much.

Look for ownership, not just obedience

Great support does not wait passively for every tiny instruction. It notices details, keeps things moving, and helps reduce friction around the founder.

  • Strong written communication
  • Calm under changing priorities
  • Good judgment about urgency
  • Reliable follow-through

Workflow fit matters more than resume volume

The right assistant for a sales-heavy founder may not be the right assistant for an operator managing multiple teams. Matching the person to the workflow matters.

The goal is leverage

A good assistant should reduce noise, improve consistency, and help the founder stay focused on higher-value work. That is the standard worth hiring for.
